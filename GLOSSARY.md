# FIMBox

FIMBox runs flood inundation jobs and supports collaboration around their results.
Workspace terminology describes the collaboration model.

## Language

**Workspace**:
A persistent collaboration space containing job history, logs, and results shared
by users with access to it.
_Avoid_: User history, history location (when referring to the collaboration space)

**Active workspace**:
The workspace a user has selected as the context for their current work.

**Job submitter**:
The user who submitted a job, regardless of which collaborators can access it.
_Avoid_: Job owner (when referring only to the person who submitted it)

**Job history**:
The retained record of jobs, their submission details when available, and their
outcomes; it is distinct from the output files produced by those jobs.
