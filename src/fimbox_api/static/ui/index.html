Remembered workspaces

Workspaces you create or open are remembered for your account in this browser. Opening checks your current access. Forget only removes the browser shortcut, not shared data.

    Create at s3://resource/<resource_id>/data/contents/<empty_subprefix>: the workspace subprefix must be a new, empty child beneath the resource’s data/contents/ (no existing files or folders). Do not choose the resource root or data/contents/ itself. Creating requires HydroShare write access. Read-only users can open workspaces and view existing results. Opening verifies the workspace with the server; job results are stored inside it.

    Click a stream to look up its NHDPlus v2.1 reach (COMID, which is the NWM ReachID) and HUC8. Flowlines appear at zoom ≥ 11; HUC8 and coarser boundaries are outlined and can take a few seconds to load. The reach must exist in the HAND hydrofabric for the job to map it.
